Good to know

Before you go ahead.

Why isn’t there a fixed price?

Your exact model, the fault, and suitable components determine the total. Ask for a device-specific quotation.

Can the estimate change?

Inspection may reveal additional damage. Any change in cost or scope should be agreed before work begins.

Does a request commit me to a repair?

No. Confirm the total, any assessment fee, parts options, and timing before proceeding.
