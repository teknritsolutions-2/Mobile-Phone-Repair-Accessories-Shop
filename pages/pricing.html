Why can the same repair cost more on another model?

Models use different components, assembly methods, and display technologies. A model-specific estimate accounts for those differences and current parts availability.

Can an estimate change after inspection?

An inspection can reveal damage that was not apparent from the initial description. Any additional work and updated cost should be discussed before proceeding.

Is diagnosis charged separately?

Ask the shop to confirm any assessment fee and how it relates to the repair estimate before agreeing to service.

Does an enquiry commit me to a repair?

No. A request begins a conversation. The repair scope and cost should be agreed before work goes ahead.
