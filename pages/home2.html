Before you book

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I get an estimate before visiting?

Share your device model and describe the problem in your request. A final estimate may require a physical inspection; any proposed work should be discussed before proceeding.

How long does a repair take?

Timing depends on the device, the fault, and parts availability. Same-day options may be possible for eligible repairs; ask for a device-specific timeframe.

Should I back up my phone?

Yes. Make a current backup where possible before service. Some troubleshooting may involve a reset, which should always be discussed with you first.

Is my preferred visit time confirmed?

No. A preferred date and time is a request. An appointment is only confirmed after the shop agrees to it.

What should I bring for collection?

Keep your repair reference and any collection instructions provided by the shop. Ask in advance if someone else will collect your device.
