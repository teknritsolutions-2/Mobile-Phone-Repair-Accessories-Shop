Before you book

A few things to know.

How long will the repair take?+

It depends on your device, the fault, and parts availability. Confirm a timeframe with the shop.

Can I get an estimate first?+

Share your exact model and symptoms. Inspection may be needed before the full cost can be confirmed.

How should I prepare my phone?+

Back up your data where possible. Bring a relevant cable or charger, and keep passwords out of the request.

Is my preferred visit confirmed?+

No. The form prepares a local request. Your visit needs separate confirmation from the shop.
