# Image sources and framing

All published photographs are real, locally hosted images. Each of the 24 photographic placements has its own source photograph. Home previews have dedicated `previewImage` fields; the Accessories catalogue uses separate `image` fields. Product detail dialogs contain the description, indicative price and enquiry action without repeating a card photograph. Logos, interface icons and the favicon are shared brand/UI elements.

Pexels photographs are covered by the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). Product photos illustrate accessory categories, not verified stock or a guarantee of a pictured model. Prices are indicative and confirmed on enquiry. No AI-generated imagery is used.
